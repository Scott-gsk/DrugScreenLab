# EXP-004 Genetic-to-Chemical Low-Data Transfer FAST Extension

## Status

`FAST_RESULT_READY_PENDING_REVIEW`

## Hypothesis

在共享 `Context + Perturbagen -> Delta978` 接口中，遗传扰动监督预训练能够在固定 chemical test 上降低化学监督需求，并在 20%/10% chemical supervision regime 保持或提高预测 ranking quality。

## Primary endpoint

固定 chemical test group 上的 Spearman：`genetic_pretrain_then_chemical - chemical_only`。

## Quantitative result

| Chemical supervision | Chemical-only Spearman | Genetic→Chemical Spearman | Primary gain |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100% | 0.0661556 | 0.0715423 | +0.0053867 |
| 20% | 0.0695154 | 0.0719153 | +0.0023999 |
| 10% | 0.0662435 | 0.0740092 | +0.0077657 |

10% direction accuracy：chemical-only `0.5186134`，genetic pretraining `0.5221751`。

## Interpretation

判定：`PROMISING_FAST`。结果支持继续做 multi-seed、cold-gene/cold-context 与 downstream reversal translation；不能作为 formal generalization、clinical efficacy 或 pathway validity 结论。10% 增益高于 20%，但 regime 间不单调，需复核。
