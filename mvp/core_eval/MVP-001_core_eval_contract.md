## 4. Observed LINCS exact-978 oracle

1. For each frozen candidate, select all `trt_cp` instances with same-plate vehicle under
   `(rna_plate, cell_id, pert_time, pert_time_unit)`.
2. Compute `Delta978 = treatment - arithmetic_mean(matched vehicles)` in the registered
   exact-978 order. Collapse technical replicates within
   `(pert_id, cell_id, pert_dose, pert_dose_unit, pert_time, pert_time_unit)` by arithmetic
   mean; each group has equal weight.
3. Aggregate each drug by coordinate-wise median across its groups. No PRISM response,
   cell line, dose, time, or observed score selects a condition.
4. For disease signature `s` and drug vector `delta_d`, freeze
   `reversal(d) = -Spearman(s, delta_d)`. Larger means stronger operational anti-correlation;
   it is not efficacy or clinical benefit.

Observed result artifact: `mvp/core_eval/observed_oracle/`;
695 matched treatment instances, 218 groups, four drugs, 60 LINCS cells, 3,439 unique
controls. All selected cache IDs were present and finite.

## 6. PRISM evaluation and decision rule

For each canonical `depmap_id`, finite rows are joined to both rankings. Because the
frozen cohort has four drugs, the low-cost MVP diagnostic uses per-line Spearman and
continuous-response top-2 overlap/NDCG@2 (NDCG@4 is also emitted). It does not claim a
paper-level top-10 result; the older ≥10-candidate top-K eligibility rule is not silently
applied to this four-drug feasibility cohort.

The predeclared feasibility signal rule is positive median per-line Spearman and
`fraction_positive_spearman > 0.5`; no CI, p-value, multi-seed selection or post-hoc
threshold is used. Current compact evaluation (33 eligible of 35 lines) gives:

- observed oracle: mean Spearman `0.4788`, median `0.8000`, positive fraction `0.7879`,
  mean top-2 overlap `0.8030`;
- predicted reversal: mean Spearman `0.4909`, median `0.8000`, positive fraction `0.8182`,
  mean top-2 overlap `0.8030`.

Therefore the frozen MVP decision is `PROMISING` / `CORE_MVP_FEASIBILITY_PROMISING`.
This is an end-to-end feasibility signal only, not a formal scientific conclusion.
